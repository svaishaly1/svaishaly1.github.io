About

Designing from
Concept to Production.

I’m an Apparel Designer with 4 years of experience across Menswear, Womenswear and Kidswear, working across both e-commerce and global export markets. I enjoy creating commercially relevant collections that balance trend, design and functionality, with experience spanning trend research, range planning and end-to-end product development. A good knowledge of graphic and print development adds another dimension to my design approach, allowing me to create distinctive visual stories across diverse categories and brands.

I specialise in Menswear, Womenswear, and Kidswear, with deep expertise in woven apparel construction, trend forecasting, and end-to-end product development. My work spans from concept and moodboards through technical design, fit, and bulk production.

I've led design for high-volume e-commerce categories at Myntra, working on brands including Nautica, French Connection, YK, Sangria, and House of Pataudi — and developed export collections for global brands at Ambattur Clothing Ltd.
