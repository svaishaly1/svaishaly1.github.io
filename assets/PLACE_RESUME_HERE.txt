RESUME PDF
==========

Place your resume PDF in the /assets/ folder.

  Filename : resume.pdf
  Full path: assets/resume.pdf

The "Download Resume" and "View Resume" buttons in the website
already point to this path. No code changes required.

If you rename the file, update these two lines in index.html:
  <a href="assets/resume.pdf" download="Vaishaly_S_Resume.pdf" ...>
  <a href="assets/resume.pdf" target="_blank" ...>

And update siteConfig.resumePdf in js/data.js:
  resumePdf: 'assets/resume.pdf',
