HERO IMAGE

  Purpose  : Full-width background for the homepage hero section
  Size     : 1920×1080px minimum — larger is better (2560×1440px ideal)
  Format   : JPG or WebP
  Content  : Editorial fashion photograph — a lookbook image, garment flat-lay,
             studio shot, or any image that communicates your design aesthetic
  Notes    : The image is darkened by an overlay so text remains readable.
             Dark-toned or moody images work especially well.

  - Delete the placeholder div above it
